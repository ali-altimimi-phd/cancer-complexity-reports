COMPUTATIONAL CANCER SYSTEMS BIOLOGY

Global Cancer Structural Inference Framework

A reproducible framework for characterizing cancer transcriptomes as organized structural systems across covariance, information, spectral, and latent-geometric representations.

Developed by Ali Al-Timimi, PhD

Cancer as a perturbation of transcriptomic organization

Cancer research commonly emphasizes altered genes, pathways, and mutations. This project asks a complementary systems-level question: does malignancy produce recurrent and reproducible reorganization of transcriptomic structure itself?

The Global Cancer Structural Inference Framework treats each tumor–normal comparison as a perturbation of organized transcriptomic state. It integrates independent mathematical engines, tests robustness across feature-space representations, synthesizes structural behavior across cancers, and projects validated structural states onto biological annotation and AI-assisted scientific reasoning.
