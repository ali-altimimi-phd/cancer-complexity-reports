Preliminary Results: BLAD/TCC Local LLM Biological Reasoning

Verified End-to-End Run of the AI-Assisted Reasoning Pipeline

Published

August 2, 2026

Purpose

This report presents actual outputs from the first clean end-to-end run of the refactored Python AI-assisted reasoning pipeline for the BLAD/TCC comparison.

The report documents operational and intermediate scientific outputs. It does not claim that the generated interpretations have been independently established as biological truth.

The evaluated workflow was:

Ontology-specific evidence briefs
        ↓
Local Gemma execution through LM Studio
        ↓
Raw response capture
        ↓
Deterministic normalization
        ↓
Canonical reasoning artifacts
        ↓
Independent artifact validation
        ↓
Priority-gated PubMed retrieval
        ↓
DuckDB persistence and benchmarking

What the Results Establish

This run establishes that the current architecture can:

  1. execute local ontology-specific reasoning;
  2. preserve raw model output;
  3. normalize variable model formatting deterministically;
  4. create canonical machine-readable reasoning artifacts;
  5. validate artifacts before retrieval;
  6. apply priority-based PubMed policy;
  7. checkpoint and persist literature retrieval;
  8. replace or append-and-deduplicate literature records;
  9. retain comparison-, ontology-, theme-, query-, and PMID-level provenance.

What the Results Do Not Establish

This run does not by itself establish:

  • that every generated theme is biologically correct;
  • that retrieved publications support the corresponding interpretation;
  • that the highest-ranked themes are the most important mechanisms in bladder cancer;
  • that the model’s mechanistic language is causal;
  • that the reasoning is stable across models, temperatures, or repeated runs.

Those questions require subsequent scientific evaluation, reproducibility testing, and possibly expert review.

Reporting Interpretation

The present results should therefore be read at three levels:

Computed evidence
Structural and gene-set results generated before the model was invoked.

AI-generated reasoning artifacts
Candidate thematic interpretations and literature queries produced from the structured evidence.

Retrieved external literature
Publication records returned by the generated queries, not yet equivalent to adjudicated support or contradiction.

Maintaining these distinctions is essential to the scientific use of the pipeline.

Next Reporting Stage

The next reporting extension should connect each theme to its retrieved PubMed records and classify the literature relationship as:

  • supporting;
  • refining;
  • challenging;
  • insufficient or unresolved.

That assessment should be explicit and traceable rather than inferred from hit counts alone.
